Section 3

Five Moody's skills

A skill is a shareable instruction file on the open SKILL.md standard. It tells the AI agent how to run a piece of analysis to a defined standard; the Moody's MCP servers connect it to the data. Moody's published its first five in June 2026, covering the workflows where its analytical frameworks are most concentrated.

SKILL 01

Earnings Call Summary

Summarizes earnings call transcripts, covering revenue trends, pricing dynamics, consumer health, tariff exposure and more.

For the desk: this week Alphabet and Amazon report, and BMW in early November. Credit-relevant lines for every corporate in the book, run across many names at once.
SKILL 02

Peer Analysis

Produces an investor-grade comparison across leverage, profitability, credit quality and more.

For the desk: BMW and Volkswagen against auto peers, or the hyperscalers side by side, as an input next to the RATES file.
SKILL 03

Public Information Book

Builds a comprehensive dossier on a single entity, spanning financials, governance, competitive landscape and risk profile.

For the desk: the first full document on an issuer with no Moody's rating, such as Pershing Square Holdings, built from Orbis financials, ownership, directors, PD and news.
SKILL 04

Rating Pitch

Generates a structured pitch deck covering sector context, rating history and peer positioning.

For the desk: the credit committee view on a new issuer before the FvS rating is signed off, with sector, history and peers in one deck.
SKILL 05

Sector Analysis

Combines Moody's proprietary research with live market intelligence to deliver a full sector-level outlook.

For the desk: the AI infrastructure and auto themes from the house view, set against Moody's sector research.
AND BEYOND

Custom skills on the same data

Because the standard is open, the team can write its own skills on top of the MCP: a daily rating-action alert across the 116 issuers in Bond Opportunities, a new-issue triage, or a RATES pre-fill that puts Moody's triggers next to the internal score. Moody's plans further skills in credit analysis, third-party due diligence and insurance underwriting.

Skill descriptions as published by Moody's, 17 June 2026. Skills are platform-agnostic and run where Moody's MCP is connected.

New issue read: Amazon comes back to the bond market

The taskDecide quickly whether a new deal from an existing issuer fits: where the rating stands, what would move it, how the market prices the credit and how much debt the issuer is adding.
What the MCP returnsRating and outlook, the opinion summary, upgrade and downgrade factors, the latest PD and CDS-implied signal and the funding figures from the filings, in one answer, each line with its Moody's document ID and date.
Ask it like thisAmazon is issuing again. Give me Moody's rating and outlook, the downgrade factors, the EDF-X PD and the CDS-implied rating with their trend this year, and how much debt Amazon has raised in 2026 from its filings. Show the source for every line.

Thin coverage: a credit view where there is no Moody's rating

The taskBuild a view on an issuer with no agency rating, which is what the in-house rating process exists for, and have an independent default signal next to it.
What the MCP returnsThe same context layer as a rated name, minus the rating: EDF-X PD and PD-implied rating, risk drivers, registry financials and ratios, ownership, directors and news. The Public Information Book skill turns it into a dossier.
Ask it like thisPershing Square Holdings has a 4.25% 2030 bond in Multi Asset Defensive. Confirm whether Moody's rates it, then give me the EDF-X PD and its trend, the ratios behind it, the ownership and the last 90 days of news.

Sector view: the AI infrastructure and auto themes

The taskTest the house themes against Moody's sector research before positioning across the bond and multi-asset funds.
What the MCP returnsMoody's sector outlooks and in-depth research for the issuer's sector, with figures and document IDs. The Sector Analysis skill combines them with live market context into one outlook.
Ask it like thisWhat does Moody's say about hyperscaler capex and how it is being financed, and what is the outlook for European automakers? Give me the key numbers and the document each comes from.

AI infrastructure

Moody's forecasts capex of about USD 785bn in 2026 and nearly USD 1 trillion in 2027 for the six largest hyperscalers, funded increasingly from debt and now equity markets. Data-centre lease commitments for the six total about USD 1.2 trillion, of which more than USD 820bn have not yet commenced. Amazon led net debt issuance among large US tech at USD 76bn over the last twelve months.

PBC_1490738 (22 Jul 2026); PBC_1494880 (22 Sep 2026); PBC_1496534 (19 Oct 2026).

Autos

Global automotive outlook negative (1 June 2026): tariffs, trade disputes and the Middle East conflict weigh on growth, with light vehicle sales about flat at 92.3 million units in 2026. Moody's sees the greatest credit pressure on European manufacturers, from tariffs, emission rules, Chinese competition and weaker China earnings. Your book holds BMW (A2 negative) and Volkswagen hybrids (Baa1 stable).

PBC_1484252 (1 Jun 2026); PBC_1490072 (3 Sep 2026).
