Regulatory backdrop — cross-cutting

Medium 2 Aug 2026 Pre-window · flag if already covered
EU AI Act — high-risk obligations now fully enforceable
From 2 Aug 2026 the EU AI Act's obligations for high-risk AI systems are binding: provider requirements (Articles 9–17) and deployer requirements (Article 26). For financial services this bites directly on AI used in credit scoring / creditworthiness assessment of natural persons, fraud detection and insurance underwriting — each now needs a risk-management system, data-governance and technical documentation, bias testing, human-oversight mechanisms and a conformity assessment before deployment. Penalties reach €15M or 3% of global turnover. New high-risk systems must comply now; legacy systems get until Feb 2027.
So what for Alvaro: this is the governance frame every European AM deploying agents is now working inside, and it's the natural bridge from last week's Anthropic Compliance-API item — position Moody's data as decision-grade, documented, auditable content that helps a deployer meet Article 26 traceability and human-oversight duties, rather than something that adds model risk. Use it as a live opener with BlackRock, Apollo and Ares InfoSec/compliance stakeholders who are scoping what "high-risk" means for their own credit and monitoring agents.
