LSEG

HIGH August 2026 PRE-WINDOW · PREVIOUSLY UNREPORTED

Private markets intelligence goes live inside Workspace — Nasdaq eVestment and Preqin data, side by side

LSEG has put private markets data directly into Workspace and Data Feeds. Both Nasdaq eVestment and Preqin datasets are now accessible in Workspace, spanning private equity, private debt, real assets, infrastructure, investor intelligence, holdings and relationships, fronted by a Private Markets Dashboard covering benchmarks, fundraising, deals, performance and investor activity. Through Data Feeds, clients also get eVestment Market Lens (28,000+ investors, 18,000+ managers, 120,000+ key contacts) and eVestment Private Market Fund Performance (~13,000 funds across PE, private debt and real assets). Jenn Giacobbe, Head of Private Markets at LSEG, frames it as broader coverage through "strategic partnerships" rather than build.

The page carries no precise publication date beyond "August 2026" — treat the day as unconfirmed; the substance is on LSEG's own site and is not in dispute.

So what for Alvaro: LSEG is now distributing Preqin content into the same European AM desks that BlackRock is trying to lock into Aladdin — which means the "who owns the private-markets view in the workflow" question is live at Permira, Hayfin and ICG next week, and the line that no incumbent has a unified private-markets picture inside the terminal no longer survives contact with a Workspace demo.
