# Investment team

Based on 8 calls with 8 firms. Titles seen: Associate; Investment Director, Growth Fund; Investment Director, software team; Partner; Principal; Vice President. Firms where this persona includes a decision maker: 1.

## What they care about
- Mapping ownership and group structures on targets quickly. At Harbourline an associate spends about a day a week working out which entity is which, and most of two days hand-building one structure chart. Veloran used the ownership data on three deals in two weeks and "it saved the team a lot of time". ([Harbourline Capital 2026-10-14](../calls/call-files/2026-10-14-harbourline-capital.md), [Aldwych Equity 2026-10-27](../calls/call-files/2026-10-27-aldwych-equity.md), [Veloran Capital 2026-10-30](../calls/call-files/2026-10-30-veloran-capital.md))
- News on small private companies, which all 3 firms that tried it in the trial found thin or empty. ([Harbourline Capital 2026-10-14](../calls/call-files/2026-10-14-harbourline-capital.md), [Veloran Capital 2026-10-30](../calls/call-files/2026-10-30-veloran-capital.md), [Tamar Equity 2026-11-09](../calls/call-files/2026-11-09-tamar-equity.md))
- Whether Moody's covers the private companies they look at: family-owned German and Dutch companies under 50 million EBITDA, German Mittelstand, Nordic private companies. ([Selby Partners (email reply)](../outbound/PE%20outreach%20tracker.xlsx), [Harbourline Capital 2026-10-14](../calls/call-files/2026-10-14-harbourline-capital.md), [Ostmark Equity 2026-11-02](../calls/call-files/2026-11-02-ostmark-equity.md))
- The health of portfolio companies' customers and suppliers. Kestrel's Principal said they do not track it at all today. ([Kestrel Partners 2026-10-21](../calls/call-files/2026-10-21-kestrel-partners.md), [Norrsten Partners 2026-10-22](../calls/call-files/2026-10-22-norrsten-partners.md), [Calder & Wye 2026-10-29](../calls/call-files/2026-10-29-calder-wye.md))
- Getting data straight into the IC memo. Harbourline wants the ownership view as a chart or table for the memo; Tamar's VP pulls financials into first drafts of IC memos by hand today. ([Harbourline Capital 2026-10-14](../calls/call-files/2026-10-14-harbourline-capital.md), [Tamar Equity 2026-11-09](../calls/call-files/2026-11-09-tamar-equity.md))
- How current the news and private company financials are. ([Kestrel Partners 2026-10-21](../calls/call-files/2026-10-21-kestrel-partners.md), [Norrsten Partners 2026-10-22](../calls/call-files/2026-10-22-norrsten-partners.md))
- Comparables for small UK companies, which take hours in Excel today. ([Calder & Wye 2026-10-29](../calls/call-files/2026-10-29-calder-wye.md))

## What to avoid
- Leading with credit risk on growth deals. Pellow Capital replied "not relevant" to the subject line "Default risk on your portcos' customers", and Norrsten said the credit angle matters less for low-debt growth companies. Norrsten pointed to EDF-X on customers and suppliers as the use that fits. ([Pellow Capital (email reply)](../outbound/PE%20outreach%20tracker.xlsx), [Norrsten Partners 2026-10-22](../calls/call-files/2026-10-22-norrsten-partners.md))
- Showing the news search on small private companies. All 3 firms that tried it in the trial found little or nothing, including French small caps at Veloran. ([Harbourline Capital 2026-10-14](../calls/call-files/2026-10-14-harbourline-capital.md), [Veloran Capital 2026-10-30](../calls/call-files/2026-10-30-veloran-capital.md), [Tamar Equity 2026-11-09](../calls/call-files/2026-11-09-tamar-equity.md))
