## HIGH — items that change what to say in the room

### BLOOMBERG — to acquire Canoe Intelligence, the private-markets data infrastructure play (29 Jul 2026)

Bloomberg is buying Canoe Intelligence, the AI-powered document-extraction platform that automates private-markets data collection for allocators. Canoe processes ~1.5M documents/month across 44,000+ funds for 500+ institutional clients representing roughly $11T AUM. Terms undisclosed. Canoe already had a certified integration with Bloomberg PORT Enterprise earlier in 2026; the deal extends that to full platform integration, feeds the ASKB agentic interface with private-markets data, and gives Bloomberg the normalised infrastructure it needs to combine public and private book views on Terminal. This is the meeting-changer of the week. Every private-markets conversation next week — HPS, Ares, Oaktree, Hayfin, Permira, Apollo, and the LP side of BlackRock — now has Bloomberg in the room as an integrated public + private + AI platform. Moody's counter is credit intelligence (ratings, PDs, scorecards, sector outlooks) that Canoe's document normalisation cannot produce. Do not walk into a private-markets meeting next week without a line on Bloomberg-Canoe. **Sources:** [Bloomberg press](https://www.bloomberg.com/company/press/bloomberg-to-acquire-canoe-intelligence-taking-a-defining-step-in-its-mission-to-transform-private-markets-investing/) · [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bloomberg-to-acquire-canoe-intelligence-taking-a-defining-step-in-its-mission-to-transform-private-markets-investing-302837466.html) · [The TRADE](https://www.thetradenews.com/bloomberg-to-enhance-private-markets-offering-through-canoe-intelligence-acquisition/) · [Wealth Management](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/alternative-investments/bloomberg-acquires-canoe-intelligence-for-ai-data)

No direct product hit on Alvaro's book next week, but private-credit or infrastructure teams at Apollo, Ares, Blackstone or BlackRock touching data-centre financing now have an incumbent-data answer from S&P. **Sources:** [SPGI press](https://press.spglobal.com/2026-07-28-S-P-Global-to-Acquire-datacenterHawk-Connecting-Global-Data-Center,-Power-and-Infrastructure-Markets) · [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sp-global-to-acquire-datacenterhawk-connecting-global-data-center-power-and-infrastructure-markets-302836389.html)

### ANTHROPIC — Claude Opus 5 released, toggleable low/medium/high effort tier, "close to Fable 5 at ~half the price" (24 Jul 2026)

First business day of the coverage window; not covered in prior brief. Opus 5 introduces an effort toggle so enterprises can dial spend against capability, and Anthropic positions it as "close to Fable 5 at ~half the price" — squarely at the enterprise concern about token burn. Better self-verification and autonomous error recovery, less back-and-forth in agentic workflows, stronger cyber and biology guardrails vs Opus 4.8. Use it as the concrete opener for anyone who paused on cost. Also directly answers the "elongating AI contract cycles" S&P called out — the price/capability curve keeps moving in the buyer's favour and Moody's benefits when the underlying model gets cheaper. **Sources:** [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/24/anthropic-debuts-claude-opus-5-with-feature-that-lets-users-toggle-between-cost-and-capability/) · [Coursiv guide](https://coursiv.io/blog/claude-opus-5)

### ANTHROPIC — MCP 2026-07-28 spec ships, stateless core plus hardened OAuth/OIDC plus enterprise-managed auth via Entra/Okta (28 Jul 2026)

Anthropic brought the MCP 2026-07-28 spec to Claude the same day the spec dropped. Three material changes: (1) stateless request/response core so servers can deploy on serverless/edge, (2) versioned Extensions framework (MCP Apps and Tasks) that adds UI and long-running workflow support without touching the core protocol, (3) production-grade OAuth 2.0 and OIDC alignment so admins can provision MCP connectors organisation-wide through Entra or Okta with zero-touch end-user setup. Intuit publicly endorsed for enterprise-scale financial workflows. Removes two of the most-heard IT/security objections to MCP-based deployment inside regulated firms — auth and stateful-server operational risk. Direct tailwind for the Moody's-native Claude / MCP pitch inside compliance, InfoSec and IT-security stakeholders at BlackRock, Apollo, Ares. The "enterprise-managed auth, no end-user setup" line is the new one for the next InfoSec call, and the "we're waiting for MCP to be enterprise-grade" objection expires this quarter. [Source: Claude release note](https://claude.com/blog/bringing-mcp-2026-07-28-to-claude)

### PERPLEXITY — Personal Computer for Windows launches, desktop agent that reads local files and routes across 20+ frontier models, aimed at finance teams (28 Jul 2026)

Windows desktop agent operating a "four-layer hybrid system" — local PC as hands, Perplexity cloud as brain — automatically selecting across 20+ frontier models with Claude Opus 4.7 as default orchestrator. Finance workflow named in the launch: pulling covenant models from Excel and lending decks from local Word without switching apps. Priced at $200/mo (Max and Enterprise Max only) — roughly 6x Microsoft Copilot's $30/user/mo. Perplexity is now selling a "read your files locally, orchestrate across every frontier model" product into the same finance-team seats Alvaro's targeting. On price it's 6x Copilot, so not a volume threat, but credible for the analyst who wants multi-model orchestration and file-level automation. Moody's-native Claude wins on decision-grade credit content, not on generic file-reading, so lead with content depth. Watch for Perplexity to name its first European AM design partner. [Source: Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321882/20260728/perplexity-brings-ai-desktop-agent-windows-routing-tasks-across-20-models.htm)
