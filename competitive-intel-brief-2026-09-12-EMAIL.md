OPENAI - HIGH - 10 Sep 2026

OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Financial Services, a tailored ChatGPT Work experience on GPT-6 Astra, designed with Morgan Stanley and Evercore, starting in investment banking and equity research. The architecture is the story.

There are three tiers, and where each provider sits matters.

Tier one, data bundled in with no separate contract: Daloopa, PitchBook, LSEG News and Crunchbase ship inside the product covering earnings transcripts, financial statements, company fundamentals and private companies. OpenAI indexes and hosts it on its own infrastructure for retrieval, latency and granular citations. Moody's is not in this tier.

Tier two, optimised MCP connectors ready for immediate use: S&P Global and FactSet named specifically. Fifty-plus connectors overall including Preqin, Datasite, Box and Intapp. Moody's is not in this tier either.

Tier three, shared sign-in and entitlement integrations: S&P Capital IQ, LSEG, MSCI, Dow Jones Factiva and Moody's. Providers recognise users through ChatGPT sign-in and auto-enable data the firm already pays for. OpenAI's wording is that it "is working with" these five, so this is in progress rather than shipped.

Admins publish firm Excel, Word and PowerPoint templates so teams turn analysis into valuation models, research notes and pitchbooks in house style. GPT-6 Astra scores 69.9% on OpenAI's OfficeQA Pro benchmark against 60.2% for GPT-5.6 Sol.

Count the tiers. LSEG appears in two, bundled news plus entitlement. S&P appears in two, entitlement plus a named MCP connector. Moody's appears in one, and it is the tier that only works for firms already holding a Moody's subscription - so it adds convenience for existing clients rather than reach to new ones.

So what: every major competitor is now inside the same OpenAI surface Moody's is in. That kills "we're natively in the agent, they aren't" as a differentiator for the second time in three weeks - the Gemini correction on 30 Aug made the same point, so this is a pattern now, not an exception. The precise, defensible line is that we are in the entitlement layer alongside S&P, LSEG and MSCI. Claim more than that and a client who has read the announcement will correct you. Then move straight to what the agent is reading rather than where it runs: ratings depth, issuer coverage, private-credit surveillance. Expect Preqin-on-OpenAI at Permira and ICG.

T. ROWE PRICE AND ANTHROPIC - HIGH - 10 Sep 2026

T. Rowe Price expanded Claude across its investment organisation. Portfolio managers and analysts on Claude and Claude Cowork in the research process, developers on Claude Code building investment tooling. The firm managed 1.87trn dollars as of 31 July 2026, roughly two-thirds retirement-related.

Three named deployment areas - research and insights, workflow support, technology enablement - under the AI leadership model announced 13 August. Vinit Agrawal leads Investment AI Solutions, Sal Dhanani Global Distribution AI, Mathieu Lorentz runs T. Rowe Price Labs, Prabhakar Bhogaraju covers Enterprise AI, Model and Technology Risk reporting to the CRO. Every application has a named business owner. CIO Eric Veiel: Claude "lets our investment professionals cover more ground and go deeper on what matters." Peter Nolan is Anthropic's head of Asset and Wealth Management. T. Rowe portfolios also invest in Anthropic.

So what: this is the reference account the AI pipeline has been missing. A named, scaled, traditional asset manager putting Claude into fundamental research with disclosed governance is exactly the proof point that unsticks pilot purgatory at Apollo, Ares and HPS. Lead with the governance structure rather than the technology - a business owner per use case and a named risk lead is the answer to the "who signs off" objection that stalls every trial.

S&P GLOBAL - HIGH - 1 Sep 2026 (pre-window, previously unreported)

Bloomberg reported on 1 September that S&P Global is evaluating separating Capital IQ Pro, potentially as a listed company valued in the high single-digit billions. No structure, timeline or formal process disclosed, and they may not proceed. It landed the same day S&P completed the sale of its upstream energy software portfolio to SLB, and follows the Mobility Global spin-off on 1 July and the MI reorg into Kensho Data & Platforms and Enterprise Solutions on 6 July.

Asymmetrix's read is that the valuation only works if the perimeter includes most of Kensho Data & Platforms - Capital IQ, Visible Alpha, With Intelligence and the Kensho AI layer - putting revenue at 2.0-2.5bn plus rather than CapIQ Pro's estimated 1bn. On that reading, With Intelligence, S&P's private markets franchise, is the growth premium that makes the story sellable. That is analyst interpretation of a press report, not company guidance - treat the perimeter as unconfirmed.

So what: a competitor publicly contemplating breaking up its data business is the strongest available argument for continuity of the Moody's platform, and it opens a fair question for any account weighing a Capital IQ renewal - who owns this contract in eighteen months. It also confirms S&P sees private markets data as its growth engine, which is the ground we are defending.
https://asymmetrixintelligence.substack.com/p/divide-and-value-s-and-p-global-weighs

LSEG - HIGH - August 2026 (pre-window, previously unreported)

LSEG has put private markets intelligence into Workspace and Data Feeds and no prior edition caught it. Nasdaq eVestment and Preqin datasets both accessible in Workspace across private equity, private debt, real assets, infrastructure, investor intelligence, holdings and relationships, fronted by a Private Markets Dashboard. Through Data Feeds, eVestment Market Lens (28,000-plus investors, 18,000-plus managers, 120,000-plus contacts) and eVestment Private Market Fund Performance, roughly 13,000 funds. Jenn Giacobbe, Head of Private Markets, frames it as coverage bought through partnerships rather than built. No day given beyond "August 2026" - exact date unconfirmed.

So what: read alongside the OpenAI launch, where LSEG News ships natively and Preqin is a connector, this is LSEG executing a coherent "our data everywhere" strategy while Moody's is one logo among thirteen. At Permira, Hayfin and ICG the private-markets conversation has to move from coverage breadth, where LSEG now wins, to credit judgement, where it does not.
