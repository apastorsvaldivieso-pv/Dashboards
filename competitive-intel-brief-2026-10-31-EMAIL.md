ANTHROPIC — MEDIUM — secondary source only — 27–28 Oct 2026

Study: in a randomised trial, 50 professional analysts sided with the position Claude had been assigned by coin flip 86% of the time (50% baseline); when the agent disagreed with an analyst's prior view, the analyst switched 60% of the time; 77% reused its arguments but only 15% credited it. Programme-evaluation work, not finance. Read via AI Weekly; Anthropic's page not opened.

So what: A governance point for credit committees at Hayfin, Permira Credit or Apollo: if analysts follow the agent, what the agent reads matters, which is the case for Moody's ratings, PDs and research as the cited source in the workflow.
https://aiweekly.co/alerts/anthropic-analysts-matched-claudes-random-lean-86-of-the-time
