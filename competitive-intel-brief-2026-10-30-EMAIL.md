S&P GLOBAL MARKET INTELLIGENCE — HIGH — UPDATE — spin-off speculation previously covered 12 Sep — 27 Oct 2026

Q3 beat, guidance raised, and a formal strategic review of Capital IQ and associated data businesses. New since September: the company itself has announced a review of alternatives for Capital IQ and associated data businesses in Market Intelligence, including possible separation. On the call, management placed the Kensho Data & Platforms data assets (financials, Visible Alpha) in scope and said RatingsDirect, RatingsXpress and Enterprise Solutions (iLEVEL, WSO) stay. The CFO cited interest from several parties, signed NDAs and engaged advisers; no timetable, first update expected on the February call. Scale cited: 15,000+ clients and 355,000 users. Q3 revenue $3.68bn vs $3.64bn expected, adjusted EPS $4.58 vs $4.42; MI +7% with a 36.2% margin; full-year EPS guidance raised to $17.70–$17.95. API call volume up nearly 50% on Q2 and 4,000%+ year on year; management said AI-rights negotiations with customers are "normalizing". Shares fell about 4%.

So what: At any account weighing Capital IQ against Moody's, ask who will own that contract and roadmap in 18 months. S&P keeps iLEVEL and WSO, so last week's private credit monitoring threat stays; the data and research layer is the uncertain part. S&P's "AI rights are normalizing" line also counters last week's Substantive Research surcharge figures if procurement at BlackRock or Apollo pushes back on Moody's AI licensing.

MORNINGSTAR / PITCHBOOK — MEDIUM — 28 Oct 2026

Q3: PitchBook growth slows to 4% with churn, while AI connector tool calls jump from 1m to 13m. Revenue +8.9% to $672.1m (6.8% organic). PitchBook $175.6m, +4.0% organic, with softness and higher churn in corporate and VC clients; licensed users +4.6%. Cumulative tool calls through the PitchBook Data Premium Connector passed 13 million in October, from 1 million in July. Morningstar Credit +18.3% to $107.8m on strong issuance. Direct licences flat. No call held.

So what: PitchBook's weakness is in corporate and VC clients, not alternative credit, so don't overplay it at Ares or Permira; expect the 13x connector usage figure when PitchBook pitches its MCP against Moody's in agentic demos.

HEBBIA — MEDIUM — single source — 23 Oct 2026

Hebbia reads clients' own Databricks data, with covenant checks against credit agreements as a named use. Data stays in the client's Databricks environment under each user's own permissions, combined with documents, filings and licensed sources; answers cite sources.
