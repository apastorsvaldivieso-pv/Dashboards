Section 3

Five Moody's skills

A skill is a shareable instruction file on the open SKILL.md standard. It tells the AI agent how to run a piece of analysis to a defined standard; the Moody's MCP servers connect it to the data. Moody's published its first five in June 2026, covering the workflows where its analytical frameworks are most concentrated.

SKILL 01

Earnings Call Summary

Summarizes earnings call transcripts, covering revenue trends, pricing dynamics, consumer health, tariff exposure and more.

For the desk: quarterly check on every corporate in the book, with the credit-relevant lines pulled out, run across many names at once.
SKILL 02

Peer Analysis

Produces an investor-grade comparison across leverage, profitability, credit quality and more.

For the desk: Nordic banks or Nordic industrials side by side on ratings, PD, ratios and ESG scores, as the starting point for relative value.
SKILL 03

Public Information Book

Builds a comprehensive dossier on a single entity, spanning financials, governance, competitive landscape and risk profile.

For the desk: the first full document on an unrated Norwegian issuer, built from Orbis financials, ownership, directors, PD and news.
SKILL 04

Rating Pitch

Generates a structured pitch deck covering sector context, rating history and peer positioning.

For the desk: the pre-investment committee view on a new issuer, sector, history and peers in one deck.
SKILL 05

Sector Analysis

Combines Moody's proprietary research with live market intelligence to deliver a full sector-level outlook.

For the desk: the sector view that frames allocation across banks, energy, industrials and forest products.
AND BEYOND

Custom skills on the same data

Because the standard is open, the team can write its own skills on top of the MCP: a daily rating-action alert for the book, a new-issue triage, a portfolio screen against the benchmark. Moody's plans further skills in credit analysis, third-party due diligence and insurance underwriting.

Skill descriptions as published by Moody's, 17 June 2026. Skills are platform-agnostic and run where Moody's MCP is connected.

The unrated universe: a credit view where there is no rating

The taskA credit view on a Norwegian issuer that has no public rating.
What the MCP returnsThe same context layer as a rated name, minus the rating: EDF-X PD and PD-implied rating, risk drivers, registry financials and ratios, ownership, directors and news. The Public Information Book skill turns it into a dossier. For unlisted issuers the private-firm PD model applies.

Live example: three unrated Norwegian names

Kongsberg Gruppen ASA

Not rated by Moody's

One-year PD 0.06%, PD-implied Aa3, public-firm model on recent good-quality inputs. FY2025: EBITDA margin 22.0%, EBITDA to finance costs 8.8x, debt to assets 6.5%, current ratio 1.2x. PD moved from 0.08% in August to 0.06% in October 2026.

Orkla ASA

Not rated by Moody's

One-year PD 0.02%, PD-implied Aa1. FY2025: EBITDA margin 16.7%, EBITDA to finance costs 13.9x, debt to assets 18.9%, current ratio 1.4x. Five-year cumulative PD 0.61%.

Mowi ASA

Not rated by Moody's

One-year PD 0.03%, PD-implied Aa2. FY2025: EBITDA margin 24.9%, EBITDA to finance costs 10.9x, debt to assets 33.9%, current ratio 2.9x. Five-year cumulative PD 0.97%.

PD-implied ratings are outputs of the EDF-X model and are not Moody's credit ratings. They are the anchor the team did not have on unrated names; the judgement still sits with the analyst. For each name the MCP also returns the ten-year PD term structure, the ranked risk drivers (asset volatility, market capitalisation, default point, liabilities) and the early warning category.

Screening new names: from the universe to a shortlist

The taskA first credit read on a set of candidate names, from the benchmark or the new-issue calendar, without doing them one by one.
What the MCP returnsA criteria search ("Baa-rated Nordic industrials", "Norwegian corporates with PD below 0.1% and no public rating") returns the candidate list. A batch run then pulls the context layer for each name into one table and flags where data is missing. The same batch pattern serves as the weekly monitor on the holdings list.

1. Define

Sector, country, rating band or PD band, size. The MCP resolves the criteria to a list of issuers rather than relying on the model's memory.

2. Enrich

Rating and outlook, PD and implied rating, key ratios, CIS, latest Moody's document date, ownership flags. Batches of 10 to 15 names per call.

3. Shortlist

Sort, filter and export to the committee paper. Each cell keeps its source ID so the figure can be checked later.

We have built this batch pattern for another European credit team: issuers processed in batches, Moody's data pulled per name, output as a locked-schema table plus a dashboard with click-through to the underlying records. It can be adapted to a Nordic universe and to Folketrygdfondet's holdings file.

Appendix

Data notes and entity resolution

  • Retrieval. All issuer data on this page was retrieved from Moody's Credit MCP on 21 October 2026. Ratings are as of that date; PD figures are as of 30 September 2026 (monthly series) with CDS-implied values to 20 October 2026; financial ratios are FY2025 unless stated.
  • Ratings versus implied ratings. "Moody's LT rating" is the Moody's Ratings long-term issuer or senior unsecured rating from the rating record. "PD-implied" is an EDF-X model output and is not a Moody's rating. For banks, the senior rating includes notches of uplift for loss given failure and government support (Nordea: a2 BCA, one notch LGF, one notch support, per the 19 October 2026 opinion); the standalone PD does not include these, which explains the gap between Aa3 and a Baa1 PD-implied rating.
  • Norsk Hydro entity resolution. Moody's rates Norsk Hydro ASA under org ID 2552, whose linked data record returned a private-firm benchmark PD with no financials. The listed Norwegian entity (register number NO914778271) carries the public-firm CreditEdge PD and FY2025 financials. The PD and ratios shown come from the listed record; the rating, opinion and ESG scores come from the rated record. The MCP shows both as alternatives; the analyst picks. This is the behaviour to expect for groups with several records, and it is why resolution is shown rather than hidden.
  • SpareBank 1 SR-Bank. The name resolves to SpareBank 1 Sør-Norge ASA (org ID 704680), the rated entity after the merger with Sparebanken Sør. The 2024 emissions record in the MCP still cites the SR-Bank annual report.
  • DNB Bank financial ratios in the EDF-X record are dated 31 December 2023; bank-format financials to FY2025 are available through the financials tool and were not pulled for this page.
  • Context layer coverage. Every node in Section 2 shows live Norsk Hydro data retrieved on 21 October 2026, with two gaps stated on the node itself: the filings service was still ingesting Hydro's annual report, and no earnings-call transcript is indexed for the entity. The news search returned six articles for the past 30 days at the significance threshold; four were about Hydro and are shown, two were unrelated and are left out. Shareholder percentages are from the Orbis register as retrieved; nominee holdings (State Street, JPMorgan, Citibank) are custodial positions, not beneficial owners. For the other nine cohort names, news, filings, ownership, directors and peers were not pulled and can be shown live in the meeting.
  • Sector outlook retrieval. The sector outlook tool for DNB returned Moody's banking research across regions (US banking system outlook, US quarterly updates, Europe banks in-depth). The Europe piece is the one used here. Sector research is keyed to Moody's sector classification, not to the issuer's country, so the analyst filters for the relevant region.
  • Covered bonds. Nordea Eiendomskreditt, the second-largest fixed income holding on the fund's published list, resolved in this MCP only to its registry record, which carries no public rating, and the covered bond programme could not be found as a deal or programme on 21 October 2026. Covered bond programme ratings are to be confirmed before relying on the MCP for that part of the book.
  • Macro data. The Macro node draws on Moody's published research (Global Macro Outlook, regional outlooks, Moody's Analytics weekly market outlook) through the document search in this Credit MCP. Moody's economic forecast databases (country-level GDP, inflation and rate series) sit in a separate Moody's server and are not shown here.
  • Folketrygdfondet figures in the opening section are from the fund's public website and a March 2026 HedgeNordic interview, not from Moody's data, and are approximate where marked.
