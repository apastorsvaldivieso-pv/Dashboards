HIGH1 Sep 2026

S&P Global explores a multibillion-dollar spinoff of Capital IQ Pro

Bloomberg reported S&P Global is weighing a separation of Capital IQ Pro — its flagship data and research platform, carrying research on 60m+ private companies — into a standalone listed company at a possible valuation in the high single-digit billions. S&P called discussions preliminary and said it may not proceed. Shares reversed an earlier loss to close up more than 3%. This is Martina Cheung's second separation in two months after Mobility Global in July, and follows the July reorganisation of Market Intelligence into Kensho Data & Platforms and Enterprise Solutions.

So what for Alvaro: a standalone CapIQ would be a pure-play data competitor sized against FactSet and LSEG's data division, and it changes the multi-year conversation at BlackRock, Apollo and Ares where CapIQ sits alongside us — a business being prepared for separation gets firmer on pricing and renewal discipline, not softer, so the "S&P will undercut us" line weakens; and there is a continuity question the buyer has not thought of yet, namely who owns the roadmap and the data rights post-separation, which nobody at S&P can answer today. It is a report, not a deal — do not overplay it.

Sources: Yahoo Finance / Investing.com · Bloomberg (original report)

HIGH1 Sep 2026

Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1

Fable 5.1 scored 52.6% on Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 against 24.7% for Fable 5, 29.0% for Claude Opus 5 and 22.4% for GPT-5.6 Sol — roughly double its predecessor. Cache reads are 75% cheaper, but higher token usage means cost per task came out about 20% above Fable 5. Fable 5.1 is generally available; Mythos 5.1 is restricted to selected US organisations under an invitation-only Life Sciences Verification Program.

So what for Alvaro: our partner's model got materially better inside the window, which is the strongest evidence yet for the April Moody's–Anthropic integration story at Apollo, Ares and HPS — use the cost note honestly rather than hiding it, because "per-task cost went up" pushes the buyer straight to the question we want, which is what grounds the reasoning. The answer to "we'll just wait for the next model" is that model quality now moves faster than any procurement cycle, and the only durable asset is the licensed content the model calls.

Sources: R&D World · 9to5Mac

HIGH1 & 4 Sep 2026

Model ML ships Fable 5.1 on day one, then benchmarks GPT-6 Astra on its own finance eval

Model ML is a London-founded agentic operating system for financial services — Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and firm systems, with workflows, document review, chat, grids and dashboards — running a model-agnostic architecture that routes each task to whichever model performs best. The Model ML Composite is their finance-specific evaluation: thirteen models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and xAI plus open-weight models from Mistral, DeepSeek, Moonshot and Z.ai, all run inside the same agent harness so only the model varies, scored by financial-services domain experts across PowerPoint and Excel creation, analytical finance, financial workflows and document retrieval, with a real dollar-per-task cost computed from published token pricing. They shipped Fable 5.1 the day it launched and had Astra benchmarked within a day.

So what for Alvaro: this is the structurally important item of the week and it cuts against how we currently tell the Anthropic story — Model ML's entire pitch is that the model is a commodity swapped per task on measured cost and performance, so at any account running it (and HSBC Asset Management is now an investor, below) "we're integrated with Claude" lands as a feature of one interchangeable component. Reframe to: the model changes every eight weeks, the licensed credit content underneath does not. Tactically, they score on dollar-per-task, so inside that harness we would be judged on token efficiency, not just answer quality.

Sources: Model ML — Introducing the Model ML Composite · Model ML blog (Astra 4 Sep, Fable 5.1 1 Sep)

HIGH24 Aug 2026PRE-WINDOW · PREVIOUSLY UNREPORTED

Rogo puts Preqin's full alternatives universe inside Felix

Felix is Rogo's agent platform, built to take a user from question to deliverable without switching systems. Preqin's alternatives universe is now queryable inside it in natural language — private equity, venture, private debt, hedge funds, real estate, infrastructure and natural resources — with fund performance, investor intelligence, manager profiles, fund terms, transactions, dry powder and ESG. Access is limited to mutual Preqin and Rogo customers and respects each firm's existing Preqin entitlements. Named use cases: deal sourcing and diligence, buyer/sponsor/LP target lists, manager due diligence, fund benchmarking, liquidity modelling, roadshow planning. Dated 24 Aug — inside the 21–28 Aug edition's window, which missed it.

So what for Alvaro: this matters more than the LSEG item below because it is BlackRock's own Preqin content being distributed through an AI-native challenger rather than through Aladdin — at Permira, Hayfin, ICG and Ares the private-markets data question is now fully commoditised at fund level, with three routes to the same content inside a workflow. Stop competing on private-markets breadth; the ground we hold is issuer- and instrument-level credit — ratings, PDs, recovery, sector benchmarks — which Preqin does not carry. Note the entitlement gating: that is the pattern to expect in our own AI licensing conversations.

Sources: Rogo — Preqin now live in Felix · Rogo newsroom

MEDIUM4 Sep 2026

OpenAI releases GPT-6 Astra

Rolling out to a limited set of organisations first, then broadly. API pricing $10 per million input tokens, $50 per million output, with a Fast Mode at double speed and double cost. Benchmarks: OSWorld 2.0 72.6% on computer use with tasks ~47% faster than its predecessor, Terminal-Bench 4.0 57.9%, GPQA Diamond 96.0%, FrontierMath Tier 4 97.6%, ARC-AGI-3 99.9%. On cyber, ExploitBench 100% and ExploitGym 42.4% — OpenAI says one configuration crosses a critical cyber threshold and access to the most advanced cyber capability is restricted at launch. Available via ChatGPT Plus/Pro/Business/Enterprise, the API, Azure and AWS Bedrock; off by default for enterprise workspaces until an admin enables it.

So what for Alvaro: the practical point is document, spreadsheet and presentation generation plus computer use — precisely the analyst workflow our buyers are automating — and published token pricing now resets the price anchor every buyer uses when weighing what our content costs inside an agent, so expect a sharper version of "the model does the analysis, why do we need the data licence".

Sources: OpenAI · 9to5Mac

MEDIUM3 Sep 2026

Clearwater Analytics: asset managers say their data is complete but not accurate

Survey of 178 senior executives across Europe, the US and Asia — insurance asset managers, hedge funds, private credit managers and generalists — fielded March 2026. 93% treat AI integration as important or critical; 73% expect AI's role in risk management to accelerate within three years; over 80% expect AI spend up at least 50% in twelve months, 22% of them by 100–299%. The line that matters: 79% call their data complete, only 56% call it accurate. Souvik Das, Clearwater CTO, on the record.

So what for Alvaro: that 79/56 gap is the cleanest third-party framing of our pitch we will get this quarter and it is the direct answer to the Model ML and Rogo items above — the constraint is not model access or workflow, it is whether the content underneath can be trusted; open with it at Apollo, Ares and HPS, and note private credit managers are explicitly in the sample so it survives a "that's a banking stat" pushback.

Source: Clearwater Analytics research release
