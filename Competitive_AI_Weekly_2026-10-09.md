## LSEG / London Stock Exchange — 8 October: DataBP AI-ready licensing intelligence (Medium)
Primary: https://www.lseg.com/en/about-us/new-at-lseg/making-market-data-administration-simpler-with-ai-ready-licensing-intelligence

LSE and DataBP announced AI-ready access to authoritative market-data licensing, usage and billing records through REST APIs and MCP. Entitlement-scoped agents can check permitted uses and reconcile invoices against licensing records. Status: partnership/capability described; broad customer deployment and realised savings not established. Named partner: DataBP. Source: LSEG primary, high quality. Quantified ROI: none.

**Commercial implication — inference:** Rights-aware machine-readable licensing could reduce friction for data-consuming agents. Moody's should treat licensing semantics and entitlement transparency as part of its agentic distribution proposition.

## OpenAI / Chatham Financial — 2 October: Capital markets validation (High)

Chatham reports trade validation falling from 30 minutes to under four minutes. Status: active customer use. Named customer: Chatham Financial. Source: OpenAI customer case study, high quality but customer-reported results.
