LSEG — HIGH — 13 Oct 2026

LSEG confirms talks to buy Octus from Permira for about $3.5bn. Responding to press speculation (Bloomberg, Reuters), LSEG confirmed it is in discussions with Permira to acquire Octus, the credit intelligence, data and workflow business (formerly Reorg/CreditSights), for approximately $3.5bn. No final agreement has been reached and any deal needs regulatory approval. LSEG describes Octus as having a leading position in illiquid and private credit, double-digit revenue growth and high retention, and says the deal would be immediately accretive to Data & Analytics revenue growth. Private Equity Wire puts Octus EBITDA at about $145m this year, rising to about $200m in 2027; Permira bought control from Warburg Pincus in 2022 at a $1.3bn valuation. LSEG shares closed down 4.7% on the day.

So what: If this closes, LSEG gets a credit-native private credit dataset already wired by MCP into Perplexity, Harvey, Rogo and now Hebbia, which turns LSEG from a weak credit competitor into a direct one at Ares, HPS, Oaktree and Hayfin; Permira is the seller, so expect the Permira conversation to touch on it, and lead with Moody's ratings, EDF-X PDs and the Allvue-based Private Credit Model as the risk layer Octus does not have.

BLOOMBERG — HIGH — 15 Oct 2026

ASKB goes generally available on the Terminal and now reads the client's own chats, watchlists and files. Bloomberg made ASKB, its conversational AI on the Terminal, generally available (beta since 24 Feb). The release adds the client's own context: plain-language questions over the user's Instant Bloomberg chats (three months of history, answers cite the source chat), over Worksheets coverage lists and watchlists, and over files uploaded to a new Bloomberg Drive, which persist across conversations, cite file and passage and stay private unless shared with colleagues. Answers involving data analysis return BQL code for Excel or BQuant. The client-content features must be switched on before use. No pricing, no named clients. Shawn Edwards, CTO: "This isn't Bloomberg building on AI. It's AI built on Bloomberg."

So what: Every target account already pays for Terminals, so the analyst who wants one agent across market data and their own documents now has a default answer without a new procurement; position Moody's agentic offer as the credit and private-company layer that plugs into whichever surface they use (including Claude and Copilot), not as a rival chat window, and ask at BlackRock and Apollo whether ASKB's client-content features have been enabled.

https://www.unite.ai/bloombergs-askb-conversational-ai-reaches-general-availability/

DEBTWIRE / OCTUS (AND HEBBIA) — MEDIUM — 13 Oct 2026

Octus credit intelligence goes live inside Hebbia via the Octus MCP Connector. Octus is connecting every Octus product a firm subscribes to into Hebbia through its MCP Connector, so a user gets one source-cited answer across expert reporting and legal analysis, private-company financials and capital structures, covenant and restructuring data, deal documents and court filings, alongside the firm's own deal history. Named workflows: comparing a covenant package with similar deals, flagging new restructuring filings and building underwriting materials. No clients named. Ben Kovacka, Head of Direct Data Services: the integration "grounds AI agents directly in proprietary credit intelligence and data". This follows Octus inside Perplexity (26 Aug), Harvey (3 Sep) and Rogo (21 Sep), all covered.

So what: Hebbia is the diligence tool at several private credit desks Alvaro covers, so Octus now occupies the credit-data slot flagged in last week's Hebbia item; ask Hebbia users at Hayfin and Oaktree whether Moody's content is connected yet, and read this together with the LSEG talks, since the same connector would come with the deal.
https://octus.com/company/news/octus-partners-with-hebbia/

S&P GLOBAL MARKET INTELLIGENCE — MEDIUM — 15 Oct 2026

Visible Alpha consensus data added to the S&P Global AI Data Portal for MCP-enabled AI tools. S&P added Visible Alpha's sell-side consensus (250+ contributing brokers, 8,000+ companies, 170+ industries) to its AI Data Portal, which serves data into MCP-enabled AI applications; the release names GPT, Gemini, Claude and Copilot. Content covers consensus estimates down to driver level with contributor counts and dispersion, standardised line items and actuals, and segment forecasts. Deterministic Retrieval (structured API queries) is live now; Adaptive Retrieval for multi-dataset workflows supports Visible Alpha later in 2026. No licensing terms or clients disclosed. Peter Licursi, Head of AI and Growth, Kensho Data & Platforms, announced it.

So what: S&P is filling its LLM data portal dataset by dataset, a week after the Capital IQ Pro AI update; expect asset managers to ask Moody's for the same choice of retrieval modes and the same list of supported models, so have the Moody's MCP coverage list and licensing terms ready rather than describing them in general terms.

ANTHROPIC — MEDIUM — 14 Oct 2026 (UNCONFIRMED — attendee-sourced)

Pre-IPO investor meeting: Q3 revenue reported at $16.8bn, up 45% on Q2, adjusted-profitable. Reuters, citing two attendees of Anthropic's 14 Oct investor meeting, reports Q3 2026 revenue of $16.8bn against $11.6bn in Q2 and $4.7bn in Q1, and that Anthropic told attendees it was profitable on an adjusted basis in Q3.
