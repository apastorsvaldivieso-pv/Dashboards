COGNITIVE CREDIT (new buy-side credit AI entrant) — MEDIUM — 23 Oct 2026

London-based Cognitive Credit raised about $40m (€35.7m) of growth investment led by Summit Partners, with existing investor ETFS Capital; Summit's Chris Bon joins the board. Its Series A in 2021 was led by Fitch Ventures. It sells corporate credit data, analytics and AI tools on 3,500+ bond and loan issuers across the US, Europe and EM, delivered by web app, Excel add-in, REST API and a Claude Connector, with every output traceable to source. Per Summit, it is used by more than 80% of the top 25 global asset managers by AUM and all top 10 debt league-table banks; active users are up 240%. No clients named. Single trade-press source (EU-Startups).

So what: This is a European credit-fundamentals specialist already sitting inside Claude at most of the big asset managers, so a credit analyst at BlackRock or Permira Credit may say "we already get issuer financials in Claude"; the Moody's answer is ratings, research, EDF-X PDs and early-warning signals in the same Claude workspace, which Cognitive Credit does not supply.
https://www.eu-startups.com/2026/10/londons-cognitive-credit-lands-e35-73-million-to-scale-its-ai-and-data-platform-for-credit-markets/

ANTHROPIC — MEDIUM — UNCONFIRMED — 23 Oct 2026

Anthropic and Hg to form a company that embeds AI engineers in European enterprises. The FT reported, citing unnamed sources, that Anthropic and London private equity firm Hg agreed to set up an independent firm that places consultants and engineers inside European businesses to deploy AI, launching by end-2026 with hubs in London and Berlin. Neither company commented. No financial-services focus stated. This follows Anthropic's May venture with Goldman Sachs, Blackstone and Hellman & Friedman.

So what: More Claude implementation capacity in London means more European firms building Claude workflows that need licensed data, which helps the Moody's MCP and Skills pitch; worth asking the Moody's Anthropic partnership team whether Moody's data can be part of that firm's standard kit before the launch.
https://pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-and-hg-form-company-to-embed-ai-engineers-in-european-enterprises

BLOOMBERG — LOW — 22 Oct 2026

Crypto Exposure Analytics now classifies holdings against the Basel Committee crypto standards. Bloomberg's tool (launched 2025) now sorts crypto and tokenised-asset exposure, including exposure hidden in funds, ETFs, derivatives, equities and bonds, into the BCBS's four categories, through Data License and the Terminal.

So what: This is aimed at bank prudential teams, not alternative asset managers, so it has no effect on Alvaro's accounts.
