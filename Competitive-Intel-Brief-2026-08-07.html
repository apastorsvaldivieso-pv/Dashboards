Fitch's private-credit default rate hits a record 6% in Q2

Fitch's trailing-12-month default rate across the ~1,300 US private-credit borrowers it tracks rose to a record 6% at end-Q2, up from the prior record of 5.7%. Fitch logged 32 default events from 20 new unique defaulters in the quarter, taking cumulative defaulters to 84. It is the highest reading since Fitch began tracking the metric, and the direction of travel — stressed-loan maturity extensions feeding the number — is the story, not the level.
