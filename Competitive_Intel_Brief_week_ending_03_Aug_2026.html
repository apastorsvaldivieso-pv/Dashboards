LSEG (London Stock Exchange Group)
MEDIUM 30 Jul 2026

H1 2026 results — "More valuable in an AI world"; Workspace embeds in Teams & Copilot, MCP channel and AI Search scaling fast

LSEG posted H1 organic growth of 8.4% and raised full-year guidance, but the read for us is the AI-distribution detail, not the print. Workspace is now embedded inside Microsoft Teams and Copilot, giving LSEG a route to ~1.5M Copilot users across its top 50 customers. AI Search is generally available and included in Workspace subscriptions (fair-use), already at ~17,000 active users; AI Deep Research is positioned as a premium add-on with ~7,000 users and adoption quadrupling since Q1. On the agentic side, 200+ customers trialled LSEG's MCP channel with tool calls up nearly 5x month-over-month, and new AI-linked product sales rose ~10%. This was missed by last week's edition, so flagging it now.

So what for Alvaro: LSEG is turning its data into agent- and Copilot-native distribution the same way we're pitching Moody's-in-Claude — at any European AM that runs Workspace (a large share of the base), the "our market data is already inside your Microsoft stack" line will surface, so lead with credit-decision depth (ratings, PDs, scorecards, committee-grade rationale) that AI Search over Workspace content cannot produce, and note the Microsoft-channel dependency where the client's AI stack is Claude/Bedrock rather than Copilot.

Sources: LSEG IR — H1 2026 interim results · Markets Media · Investing.com — H1 slides · BigGo earnings recap

Ellis (new private-credit AI entrant)
MEDIUM 30 Jul 2026

Emerges from stealth with $10M+ seed to build "the AI-native operating foundation for private credit"

Ellis launched publicly with more than $10M in seed funding led by First Round Capital (645 Ventures, Khosla Ventures, Harlem Capital, Slow Ventures and others participating; angels include Ariel Alternatives' Mellody Hobson, Thrive's Josh Kushner and Mercury's Immad Akhund). Founder Ryan Williams previously built Cadre. The pitch is AI agents that take on the fragmented back- and middle-office workflow private credit managers live in — documents, spreadsheets, correspondence — positioning as an operations layer, not a data or ratings provider. This is the second private-credit-specific AI entrant to surface in two weeks after Allvue's benchmarks launch.

So what for Alvaro: Not a credit-intelligence threat and it's seed-stage, but it's another name aimed squarely at the GP private-credit workflow that's a firm priority — worth a one-line awareness with HPS, Ares, Oaktree, Hayfin and Permira so you're the one who framed it: Ellis automates operations, Moody's supplies the decision-grade credit content those agents still need to reason over. Watch for its first named GP design partner.

Sources: BusinessWire — Ellis press release · TechCrunch · Alternative Credit Investor

S&P Global — pre-window, previously unreported
HIGH PRE-WINDOW · 21 Jul · not in prior briefs 21 Jul 2026

Adaptive Retrieval launched — customer AI agents get natural-language access to licensed, audited S&P data (the S&P Global AI Data Portal)

Dated 21 Jul, so strictly outside this window, but neither the 25 Jul nor the 31 Jul brief caught it and it matters too much to leave buried. S&P launched Adaptive Retrieval: customer LLMs and AI agents can pull and assemble licensed S&P Global data through natural-language queries across multiple datasets, with citations and auditability, aimed at multi-step research and report generation. It pairs with Deterministic Retrieval (the Kensho LLM-ready API, live since 2025); together they're packaged as the S&P Global AI Data Portal. This is S&P productising exactly the "your agents query our audited data directly" motion — the commercial heart of the AI data-licensing question.

So what for Alvaro: This is the direct competitive answer to the Moody's-native-Claude / MCP data-access story at BlackRock, Apollo and Ares — when procurement or a data team asks "how do our agents get to your data, and can we audit it," S&P now has a named, shipped product, so make sure the Moody's MCP / agent-access and data-licensing answer is equally crisp and, ideally, first in the room before this gets cited against us.

Sources: S&P Global press · PR Newswire
