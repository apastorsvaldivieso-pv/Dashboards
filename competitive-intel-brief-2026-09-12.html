OpenAI

HIGH 10 Sep 2026

ChatGPT for Financial Services launches — with Moody's, S&P, LSEG and MSCI inside it

OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Financial Services, a tailored ChatGPT Work experience running on GPT-6 Astra, built with Morgan Stanley and Evercore as design partners. It starts in investment banking and equity research. The commercially important part is the data architecture:

  • Tier 1 — data bundled in, no contract required: Daloopa, PitchBook, LSEG News and Crunchbase ship inside the product — earnings transcripts, financial statements, company fundamentals, private companies. OpenAI indexes and hosts this on its own infrastructure for retrieval, latency and granular citations. Moody's is not in this tier.
  • Tier 2 — optimised MCP connectors, ready for immediate use: S&P Global and FactSet named specifically; 50+ connectors overall including Preqin, Datasite, Box and Intapp. Moody's is not in this tier either.
  • Tier 3 — shared sign-in and entitlement integrations: S&P Capital IQ, LSEG, MSCI, Dow Jones Factiva and Moody's. Providers recognise users via ChatGPT sign-in and auto-enable data the firm already pays for. OpenAI's wording is that it "is working with" these five — so this is in progress, not shipped.
  • Artifact generation: admins publish firm Excel, Word and PowerPoint templates; teams turn analysis into valuation models, research notes and pitchbooks in house style. GPT-6 Astra scores 69.9% on OpenAI's OfficeQA Pro benchmark against 60.2% for GPT-5.6 Sol.

Count the tiers. LSEG appears in two (bundled news plus entitlement). S&P appears in two (entitlement plus a named MCP connector). Moody's appears in one — and it is the tier that only works for firms already holding a Moody's subscription, so it adds convenience for existing clients rather than reach to new ones.

So what for Alvaro: Every major competitor is now inside the same OpenAI surface Moody's is in, which kills "we're natively in the agent, they aren't" as a differentiator for the second time in three weeks — the Gemini correction on 30 Aug made the same point, and this is now a pattern, not an exception. The precise, defensible line is "we're in the entitlement layer alongside S&P, LSEG and MSCI" — claim more and a client who has read the announcement will correct you. Then move straight to what the agent is reading rather than where it runs: ratings depth, issuer coverage, private-credit surveillance. Expect Preqin-on-OpenAI to come up at Permira and ICG.

Sources: OpenAI announcement · CNBC · Unite.AI

Anthropic / T. Rowe Price

HIGH 10 Sep 2026

T. Rowe Price puts Claude into the investment process across a $1.87trn book

T. Rowe Price and Anthropic announced the expansion of Claude across the asset manager's investment organisation. Portfolio managers and analysts are using Claude and Claude Cowork in the research process; developers are using Claude Code to build investment tooling. The firm managed $1.87trn as of 31 July 2026, roughly two-thirds retirement-related.

Three named deployment areas: research and insights (Cowork synthesising complex information), workflow support (multi-step knowledge-intensive processes), and technology enablement (Claude Code). It runs under the AI leadership model announced 13 August — Vinit Agrawal on Investment AI Solutions, Sal Dhanani on Global Distribution AI, Mathieu Lorentz running T. Rowe Price Labs, Prabhakar Bhogaraju on Enterprise AI, Model and Technology Risk reporting to the CRO. Every application has a named business owner. CIO Eric Veiel: Claude "lets our investment professionals cover more ground and go deeper on what matters." Peter Nolan is Anthropic's head of Asset and Wealth Management. T. Rowe Price portfolios also invest in Anthropic.

So what for Alvaro: This is the reference account the AI pipeline has been missing — a named, scaled, traditional asset manager putting Claude into fundamental research with disclosed governance, which is exactly the proof point that unsticks pilot purgatory at Apollo, Ares and HPS. Use the governance structure, not the technology, as the talking point: a business owner per use case and a named risk lead is the answer to the "who signs off" objection that stalls every trial.

Sources: T. Rowe Price investor relations · Unite.AI

S&P Global

HIGH 01 Sep 2026 PRE-WINDOW · PREVIOUSLY UNREPORTED

Bloomberg: S&P Global weighing a Capital IQ spin-off at high single-digit billions

Bloomberg reported on 1 September that S&P Global is evaluating separating Capital IQ Pro, potentially as a listed company valued in the "high single-digit billions". No structure, timeline or formal process disclosed, and S&P may not proceed. The report landed the same day S&P completed the sale of its upstream energy software portfolio to SLB, and follows the Mobility Global spin-off (1 July) and the Market Intelligence reorg into Kensho Data & Platforms and Enterprise Solutions (6 July).

Analysis from Asymmetrix argues the valuation only works if the perimeter includes most of Kensho Data & Platforms — Capital IQ, Visible Alpha, With Intelligence and the Kensho AI layer — putting revenue at $2.0–2.5bn+ rather than CapIQ Pro's estimated ~$1bn. On that read, S&P's private markets franchise, With Intelligence, is the growth premium that makes the story sellable. Note this is analyst interpretation of a press report, not company guidance — treat the perimeter as unconfirmed.

So what for Alvaro: A competitor publicly contemplating breaking up its data business is the strongest available argument for continuity of the Moody's platform, and it opens a legitimate question to put to any account weighing a Capital IQ renewal — who will own this contract in eighteen months. It also signals S&P sees private markets data as its growth engine, which is the exact ground Moody's is defending.

Sources: Asymmetrix Intelligence analysis · report summary (original: Bloomberg, 1 Sep 2026)

LSEG

HIGH August 2026 PRE-WINDOW · PREVIOUSLY UNREPORTED

Private markets intelligence goes live inside Workspace — Nasdaq eVestment and Preqin, side by side

LSEG has put private markets data directly into Workspace and Data Feeds. Both Nasdaq eVestment and Preqin datasets are accessible in Workspace across private equity, private debt, real assets, infrastructure, investor intelligence, holdings and relationships, fronted by a Private Markets Dashboard. Through Data Feeds clients also get eVestment Market Lens (28,000+ investors, 18,000+ managers, 120,000+ key contacts) and eVestment Private Market Fund Performance (~13,000 funds). Jenn Giacobbe, Head of Private Markets at LSEG, frames it as coverage bought through partnerships rather than built. LSEG's page carries no day beyond "August 2026" — treat the exact date as unconfirmed.

So what for Alvaro: Read alongside the OpenAI launch, where LSEG News ships natively and Preqin is a connector, this is LSEG executing a coherent "our data, everywhere" strategy while Moody's is one logo among thirteen — so at Permira, Hayfin and ICG the private-markets conversation has to move from coverage breadth, where LSEG now wins, to credit judgement, where it does not.

Source: LSEG Workspace updates
