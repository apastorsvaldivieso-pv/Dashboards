## HIGH — items that change what to say in the room

### ALLVUE SYSTEMS — private-credit benchmarks from deal data (21 Jul 2026)

Allvue debuted OneVue, its next-gen platform, together with Portfolio Intelligence (AI-powered workspace) and Deal Analytics — the industry's first private-credit benchmarks built from first-party, rights-cleared, anonymised deal-level data drawn from Allvue's own operational footprint. ~150,000 assets and securities, 200+ KPIs. Target buyer is the GP credit portfolio team; they cite their own GP survey where 44% of PMs name benchmarking as a top tech need. This is a direct hit on the Moody's private-credit intelligence pitch inside HPS, Ares, Oaktree, Hayfin and Permira. The counter Moody's has to lead with next week is that Allvue's edge is proprietary deal data derived from admin/software workflows — it is not credit-quality intelligence. Moody's plays on ratings, PDs, macro scenarios, sector outlooks and ESG credit signals that Allvue cannot manufacture from admin data alone. Expect this to come up in every GP meeting; pre-empt it. **Sources:** [Markets Media](https://www.marketsmedia.com/allvue-debuts-first-benchmarks-built-from-private-credit-deal-data/) · [Alternative Credit Investor](https://alternativecreditinvestor.com/2026/07/20/allvue-systems-launches-private-credit-benchmarks-based-on-deal-data/) · [MarTech Series](https://martechseries.com/analytics/allvue-debuts-onevue-with-the-launch-of-portfolio-intelligence-and-deal-analytics-the-industrys-first-benchmarks-built-from-private-credit-deal-data/)

### MSCI — first AI training-data licence signed (21 Jul 2026)

On the Q2 2026 earnings call, MSCI disclosed it has signed its first AI training-data licence — a client is now permitted to train models on MSCI content. Management framed this as "a growing area of opportunity"; no client name or price disclosed. This is the market-structure event of the week for anyone selling licensed financial data. Procurement teams at BlackRock, Apollo, Ares and HPS will use it to open the same conversation with Moody's: separate licence, separate price, separate audit trail for LLM training vs. use. Alvaro needs a Moody's line on this before next week's renewal conversations — what MA will and won't license for training, and at what tier — otherwise this becomes a defensive scramble mid-renewal. **Sources:** [MSCI Q2 highlights](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/msci-inc-msci-q2-2026-230034304.html) · [Full transcript](https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/07/60586606/transcript-msci-q2-2026-earnings-conference-call)

### S&P GLOBAL MARKET INTELLIGENCE — Farsight partnership adds AI client-materials to Capital IQ Pro (23 Jul 2026)

SPGI Market Intelligence and Farsight announced an AI-native workflow capability embedded inside Capital IQ Pro that generates pitch decks, CIMs and valuation materials from a firm's own templates and prior work. Positioned as an add-on to Capital IQ Pro, delivery "later this year." Announcement sits within the wider Kensho Data & Platforms narrative and follows the 6 July operating-model reshape that stood up an "Agentic Solutions" leadership. Sally Moore (CCO) fronts the release. This is the "why not just use Capital IQ?" answer prospects will now cite. Alvaro's counter in front of any Capital IQ-heavy European AM (which is most of them at the base layer): Farsight is a workflow layer over generic financial data, not decision-grade credit intelligence; Moody's plays where the answer must survive a credit committee, not a pitch pack; SPGI is stacking third-party AI on top of Kensho, while Moody's is embedding data directly into the LLMs where analysts already work (Claude, MCP). **Sources:** [SPGI press release](https://press.spglobal.com/2026-07-23-S-P-Global-Market-Intelligence-Partners-with-Farsight-to-Launch-AI-powered-Financial-Workflow-Capability-for-Capital-IQ-Pro) · [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sp-global-market-intelligence-partners-with-farsight-to-launch-ai-powered-financial-workflow-capability-for-capital-iq-pro-302832752.html) · [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/sp-global-partners-with-farsight-for-ai-tool-in-capital-iq-pro-93CH-4808653)

### LSEG — LSE 24 announced: 24/5 trading venue with "agent-based trading" connectivity (21 Jul 2026)

London Stock Exchange announced LSE 24, a Monday-to-Friday near-continuous trading venue. ETPs first (launch H1 2027 subject to regulator), equities next. LSEG explicitly frames the venue as having "native connectivity for the next generation of agent-based trading capabilities"; client testing available by end-2026. Not a direct hit on Moody's data/analytics business, but useful colour. Talking point in front of clients measuring vendors on how seriously they are treating agentic workflows — Moody's parallel story (agentic risk intelligence via Claude, MCP) plays well and is available today rather than in H1 2027. **Sources:** [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/london-stock-exchange-launch-lse-063000001.html) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321141/20260721/london-stock-exchange-builds-ai-native-24-5-trading-venue-chain-settlement-path.htm) · [Genfinity](https://genfinity.io/2026/07/22/london-stock-exchange-lse-24-overnight-trading-venue-2027/)
